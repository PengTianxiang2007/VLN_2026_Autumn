Write one concise outbound route description in English. Describe the following in chronological order:
1. Where the robot was at A, including visible landmarks and their spatial relationship to the robot.
2. How the robot moved from A toward B. Connect forward motion and turns to visible changes, such as passing a doorway, entering a corridor, or turning beside an identifiable object.
3. How the robot approached B and what distinguishes the area around B.

Describe left and right from the robot's outbound heading at that moment. Distinguish seeing a landmark from passing it. Use the action log for action order and the images for evidence of physical progress. If the images do not establish that the robot passed a landmark or crossed an opening, describe only what is visible. A recorded STOP means the outbound robot issued STOP at that point.

Describe the route that actually happened. The return controller will choose its own actions later.

Return one JSON object with a single string field:
{"route_description":"..."}
