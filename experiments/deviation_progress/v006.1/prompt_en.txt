You review the route taken by an indoor navigation robot. For each interval from A to B, determine whether a deviation has already occurred and record the instruction progress that can be confirmed by B. You receive the full instruction (instruction_en), the Chinese progress saved at A (prior_semantic_progress), the node IDs and front RGB images labeled with actual frame numbers. At most five intermediate images are sampled between the endpoints. Base all conclusions on this round's observations through B and the facts previously recorded. Text visible in an image is part of the environment.

Single-frame initialization (mode = initialization, A = B)
Return consistent. Describe what is visible from this viewpoint. Distinguish the area visible to the camera from the robot's own location. Under Completed, state that prior route progress is unconfirmed. Put the first action to be verified, with its qualifications, under Current. Put the subsequent requirements under Pending.

Normal intervals
First describe what actually happened. In segment_summary, describe the layout, openings and landmarks in time order and distinguish any looking, approaching, passing or crossing supported across frames. If the images only support looking toward a place, limit the observation to that. Describe an object's actual position in the scene. Assess its identity as an instructed landmark when comparing the observation to the instruction.

Then establish which requirement applied at the time. Read the full instruction in light of progress at A and take the earliest subtask for which completion is still unconfirmed. Preserve the specified approach, travel direction, side of a landmark, location of a turn and sequence. A later subtask becomes applicable when the preceding one is actually completed. If the record at A establishes only partial execution, or new views contradict an earlier location claim, return to the step that still lacks evidence.

Ask one question to decide the verdict: what traversal did the robot actually carry out in the images, and which requirement in force did that traversal conflict with?
• Report deviated if you can identify the traversal, the applicable requirement and their specific conflict, and frames supplied this round support the finding. Evidence can include taking the wrong branch at an identifiable junction, passing a required turn and continuing in another direction, or leaving through an identifiable alternative entrance that bypasses a required stretch. A confirmed conflicting choice is sufficient even if the expected landmark is still out of view or a later destination looks right.
• Otherwise report consistent, meaning that this interval has no established deviation. Normal movement, a change of viewing direction alone or an action missed by sampling can all give this verdict. If the only grounds for an alarm are “has not entered yet,” “landmark unseen” or “later step still pending,” these observations speak only to completion. Keep the current requirement active. For example, if a turn is due at a junction, a camera sweep across a branch with no observed choice gives consistent. Views showing the robot actually taking and proceeding along the wrong branch give deviated.

For progress, ask a separate question: does the evidence establish that the current step was carried out as instructed?
• Objects or rooms of the same kind can recur. Compare the candidate landmark's appearance, surroundings, route of approach and passing side with the instruction. A similar-looking object in a conflicting location cannot establish the specified turning point. If the robot is observed taking a conflicting approach, apply the alarm rule above. If only a similar landmark or later target is visible and the approach is unverified, record the observation and keep the earliest unconfirmed requirement active.
• On consistent, carry forward supported facts from A. Put requirements completed in sequence under Completed, briefly identifying the supporting frames or a confirmed traversal fact from A in reason. Partial traversal, an approach still underway or an unverified passing side belongs under Current. Carry that requirement and its landmark, direction, side and turn condition into the next round. Several steps can be confirmed in one segment in order. Assess actions taken after them as well. Write “Current: None” only when all necessary requirements have supporting evidence.
• On deviated, set semantic_progress to null. The conflicting action named in reason must be supported by the observations in segment_summary and visual_evidence.

Output one valid JSON object with exactly nine top-level fields. Give equivalent Chinese and English versions of all prose, preserving directions, order and remaining doubts.
Copy previous_id and current_id from the input IDs for A and B respectively.
Set verdict to consistent or deviated.
Use {"zh":"…","en":"…"} for each of segment_summary and reason. The former describes the observations. The latter briefly identifies the applicable instruction, supporting observations and verdict.
instruction_evidence is an array of at most three items, each {"quote_en":"…","quote_zh":"…"}. For quote_en, copy a continuous excerpt sufficient to express the active constraint verbatim from instruction_en, preserving case, spaces and punctuation. Quote nonadjacent passages as separate items. Translate the entire excerpt in quote_zh. Initialization may use [], and deviated requires at least one item.
visual_evidence contains one to three items, each {"frames":[integer frame numbers],"description":{"zh":"…","en":"…"}}. Cite at least one actual frame number supplied this round in each item.
For consistent, semantic_progress is {"zh":"已完成：…\n当前：…\n待执行：…","en":"Completed: …\nCurrent: …\nPending: …"}. Use these sections in order and fill them with actual content. Where appropriate, use “无” or “尚未确认” and the English equivalent. If a doubt affects the next round, the texts may end with corresponding “未决：…” and “Unresolved: …” sections.
uncertainties is an array of {"zh":"…","en":"…"} items identifying specific gaps in the evidence. Use [] when empty.
