ADDITIONAL DEFAULT-SUSPICION RULE
For a restricted or partially visible side, ask whether that side could
continue even though its destination is not visible.

Treat the side as POSSIBLE whenever a door frame, wall edge, recess,
cabinet end, occluding boundary, or image edge leaves unclassified space
or floor continuity that could support an enterable route. A visible wall
surface alone does not establish BLOCKED unless its boundaries and its
barrier to local passage are sufficiently visible to exclude that route.

When the visual evidence merely does not rule out a side route, choose
POSSIBLE over BLOCKED. This is a default for unresolved local space, not
permission to invent a complete route.

If this changes a side from BLOCKED to POSSIBLE, apply the scoring rules:
the score must be at least 2, and at least 3 when another route is
already PASSABLE.
