
Apply the definition by route connectivity, NOT by a stereotypical junction shape.
A straight continuation PLUS an accessible open side doorway or side passage are TWO
onward routes after excluding the incoming path. A traversable opening into a room
can be a branch even without a door frame. Do not reject it merely because the main
corridor continues straight, one route looks dominant, or the destinations are different
functional areas. Conversely, two views into the same uninterrupted walking passage
are not two routes; require distinct traversable openings or separated passages.
Before deciding status, enumerate the visibly supported passages and exclude the
incoming route using observed motion. Check that the final decision agrees with this
count. If two distinct onward routes are accessible HERE, report crossing even when
there is no T or cross shape. If those choices are still beyond a narrow approach,
report crossing AHEAD rather than claiming they are already HERE. A closed door must
not erase other open routes. Never assume an unseen opening or a passable furniture gap.
